# The Mentalist
About the Series
The Mentalist is an American crime drama television series that premiered in 2008. It follows Patrick Jane, a highly observant man who uses his exceptional skills in reading people's behavior and noticing small details to help solve criminal cases.

## Main Character
### Patrick Jane 
– A former psychic medium who uses his intelligence, observation skills, and psychological tricks to solve crimes. He works as a consultant for the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI).
